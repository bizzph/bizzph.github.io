General settings

Fee defaults affect new transactions only. Auto-lock and the optional local PIN apply to this browser/device.

Network: live pricing is off by default. When enabled, Trade Vault can connect only to the public Coins.ph market WebSocket and sends pair symbols only. No analytics, telemetry, account API, or transaction upload is built in.

Locks after inactivity and uses the same limit when the app is backgrounded.
BUY fees are calculated from the executed base quantity. SELL fees are calculated from the quote total. You can still override the exact exchange-reported fee on each transaction.
4-digit PIN Checking…
